## Full description
Lemon SSH Client is a fast, simple terminal for managing your servers from your Android phone.

• SSH, Telnet and local shell in one app
• Saved connections, frequently used list and quick connect
• Password and SSH key authentication; generate Ed25519 keys or import your own
• Trusted host-key verification
• Local and remote port forwarding
• Snippets for commands you use often
• Extra key bar: ESC, TAB, CTRL, ALT, FN, arrows, HOME/END, PgUp/PgDn
• Pinch to zoom, adjustable font size and color themes
• Sessions stay connected in the background
• Backup and restore of your connections
• No ads, no tracking, no accounts

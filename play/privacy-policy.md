# Privacy Policy – Lemon SSH Client

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Lemon SSH Client ("the app") is an SSH, Telnet and local-shell client. It is developed by Stupovi Studio.

**Data we collect:** none. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers of our own. We do not receive, store or share any of your data.

**Data stored on your device:** connection profiles (host, port, username), SSH keys, snippets, port-forward settings and trusted host keys are stored only on your device. Passwords and private keys are kept in Android's encrypted secure storage. Backups you export are created only when you ask and are placed in your clipboard; you decide where to keep them.

**Network:** the app connects only to the servers you configure, using SSH or Telnet. Telnet is not encrypted; use it only on networks you trust.

**Permissions:** Internet (to connect to your servers); foreground service and notifications (to keep your sessions connected in the background and show that they are active); wake lock (to keep connections alive while the screen is off); battery-optimization exemption (optional, so Android does not close sessions).

**Children:** the app is not directed at children.

**Deleting data:** uninstalling the app removes all data stored by it.
